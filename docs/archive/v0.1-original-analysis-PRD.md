## 1. Overview & Problem

Chemical procurement in India today happens over phone, WhatsApp, and email. A buyer who needs (say) 10,000 kg of Toluene at 99% purity calls around to suppliers, collects quotes informally, haggles, and has no clean audit trail of who quoted what. Sellers, meanwhile, broadcast availability blindly and get spammed with irrelevant requests.

This platform replaces that with a **verified, structured reverse-auction marketplace**: buyers post a precise requirement, qualified sellers bid competitively (and blindly), and a two-stage negotiation loop settles on a price. Identity is anchored to government GST registration so every party is a real, licensed business.

**What this product is:** a matchmaking + negotiation + record-keeping layer.
**What this product is *not* (at least in v1):** a payment processor, an escrow service, or a logistics provider. Money and goods move off-platform. _(See §8 and ⚠️ DECISION NEEDED in §10.)_

## 2. Goals, Non-Goals & Success Metrics

### Goals
1. Let verified buyers run a structured reverse auction for a specific chemical in under 5 minutes.
2. Ensure only *relevant, qualified* sellers are notified of each request (CAS + grade + role filtering).
3. Give buyers a clear, comparable view of all bids across both negotiation stages.
4. Maintain a tamper-proof audit trail of who bid, countered, accepted, and closed each deal.

### Non-Goals (v1)
- On-platform payments, invoicing, or escrow.
- Logistics / shipment tracking.
- International trade (currency, customs). Domestic India only.
- Native mobile apps (responsive web only — see §7).

### Success Metrics _(⚠️ targets need owner sign-off)_
- **Activation:** % of registered companies that publish ≥1 auction within 14 days.
- **Liquidity:** median number of qualified bids per auction (a marketplace dies below ~3).
- **Completion:** % of auctions reaching "Confirm Deal" vs. expiring.
- **Time-to-close:** median hours from auction launch to deal confirmation.
- **Retention:** repeat auctions per buyer per month.

## 4. Glossary (terms the spec uses but never defines)

- **Department** — used in the catalog uniqueness rule ("one user per department") but never created or assigned anywhere in onboarding. ⚠️ Where does a department come from?

## 5. Functional Requirements

### 5.1 Company Onboarding & Identity

**FR-1.1 GST-verified registration.** On signup, the company enters a GST number. The system calls a GST verification API to fetch the legal name and registered address, which become the locked corporate identity.
- ⚠️ **Which GST API?** GSTN doesn't expose this directly to consumer apps — you go through a GST Suvidha Provider (GSP) or a third-party (ClearTax, Masters India, Signzy, Surepass). Decide vendor, cost-per-call, and rate limits.
- ⚠️ **Multi-GSTIN companies.** One PAN can have many GSTINs (one per state). Is each GSTIN a separate account, or do they roll up under one company? This is the single most common real-world onboarding edge case in India.
- ⚠️ **API downtime fallback.** If the GST API is down, can a user register provisionally, or is signup blocked?
- **FR-1.2** One primary account per unique GST. Duplicate attempts blocked.
- **FR-1.3** Corporate name locked; editable once per calendar year. ⚠️ Mechanism unclear — admin self-edit, support ticket, or auto re-fetch from GST?
- ⚠️ **First-admin establishment.** How is the very first admin verified — email link, phone OTP? Source doc is silent.
- ⚠️ **Account recovery.** Password reset, admin offboarding, "admin left the company" flows — all unspecified.

**FR-1.4 Sub-user creation.** Admin creates team members with First/Last name, corporate email, phone, designation. Each gets a unique User ID + password.
- Names and designations are freely editable (promotions); corporate identity is not.
- ⚠️ Password policy, 2FA, and session/timeout rules are undefined (see §7).

### 5.2 Catalog & Product Listing

**FR-2.1 Per-product role declaration.** For each listed chemical, the seller checks Manufacturer / Distributor / Trader (multiple allowed). This drives buyer-side filtering.

**FR-2.2 CAS → name auto-fill via PubChem.** User enters a CAS number; the app queries PubChem REST and populates the scientific name.
- ⚠️ **PubChem reliability risk.** PubChem is not natively keyed by CAS — CAS appears as a *synonym*, and lookups can return zero, one, or multiple compounds, plus deprecated/retired CAS numbers. Define behavior for not-found and ambiguous matches, and a manual-override path. Consider caching a CAS↔name table to reduce API calls and handle outages.

**FR-2.3 Custom mixtures (N/A track).** Selecting "N/A" unlocks a free-text name field for blends ("80% Ethanol / 20% Water").

**FR-2.4 Quality grade.** Every item categorized as Distilled / Pure / Trade (see glossary ⚠️).

**FR-2.5 Cross-user uniqueness.** Within one GST, a CAS number can be held by only one user *per department*. Collision shows: *"This product is already managed by [Name] ([Designation]) — [contact]."*
- Bilateral exception: same CAS may exist once in a sales profile and once in a purchase profile.
- ⚠️ Depends entirely on the undefined "department" concept.

**FR-2.6 Seller outreach throttling.** One proactive share per CAS per 7-day rolling window. Blocked attempts show the exact reset time. Privacy toggle: Send to All / Send to Registered Only.
- ⚠️ Is the 7-day window rolling or fixed-calendar-week? "Per calendar week" and "7 days" in the source conflict.

### 5.3 Purchaser Request (Auction) Creation

**FR-3.1 Request form fields:** validated CAS (locks name in bold), quantity (kg) + minimum purity %, packing type, delivery address (default registered, or custom), logistics basis (Delivered / Ex-Works), supplier-type filter (Mfr/Dist/Trader), one technical-spec attachment (PDF/JPG), plain-text remarks, closing date/time.
- ⚠️ **Units.** kg only — but chemicals often trade in metric tons or litres. Confirm scope.
- ⚠️ **Closing timer bounds.** Min/max auction duration? Can it be extended? Timezone = IST assumed but should be explicit.
- ⚠️ **Attachment rules.** Size limit, virus scanning, single-file-only (the spec says one file), and whether PNG is allowed.

**FR-3.2 Registered network.** Buyers register partners by GST + a specific CAS (a multi-product partner is registered once per chemical).
- ⚠️ What if the partner GST isn't on the platform yet — invite flow? Can you register a non-existent GST?
- ⚠️ What if a registered partner has blocked this buyer (see §5.4)? Conflict resolution undefined.

**FR-3.3 Privacy toggle + empty-network safeguard.** Send to All / Registered Only. If "Registered Only" with zero partners for that CAS, publication is blocked with the prompt to register a vendor or switch to All.

**FR-3.4 Targeting algorithm.** Two-tier match on CAS + quality grade; for N/A mixtures, tokenize the text and match any token (IPA OR Toluene). Then drop vendors whose declared role the buyer didn't select. Qualified vendors get in-app pop-up + email.
- ⚠️ Token matching on free text is noisy ("IPA" matches "tdIPArt"?). Define tokenization rules and false-positive handling.

### 5.4 Seller Interaction & Bidding

**FR-4.1 Stage-1 gateway.** On opening a request, the seller sees basic requirements (pricing form locked) and must pick: **Accept & Quote** (unlocks specs + bidding), **Ignore** (mutes this auction), or **Block This Purchaser** (this-CAS-only or all-requests). The same three-button gate applies to buyers when a seller proactively shares.

**FR-4.2 Pre-qualification view.** After accepting, the header shows buyer's corporate name + acting user + designation, and the seller can open the spec document before pricing.

**FR-4.3 Split pricing engine.** Seller enters Basic Rate/kg + Freight Rate/kg (₹); app auto-sums to Total Rate/kg.
- ⚠️ **Ex-Works vs Freight.** If the basis is Ex-Works (buyer arranges pickup), should freight be forced to ₹0 / hidden? The interaction between FR-3.1's logistics basis and the freight field is unspecified.

**FR-4.4 Partial shipment bids.** A seller may bid on less than the full quantity (e.g., 5,000 of 10,000 kg).
- 🚨 **BIGGEST UNRESOLVED ISSUE — see callout below.**

**FR-4.5 COA upload.** Seller must upload a Certificate of Analysis.
- ⚠️ What about make-to-order chemicals where no COA exists yet?

**FR-4.6 Blind rank.** If the buyer enabled it, the seller sees only their live rank (#3), based on lowest Total Rate/kg. Competitor identity and prices are masked. Bids can be revised until close.
- ⚠️ **Ties.** Two identical total rates — same rank, or tiebreak by timestamp/quantity?
- ⚠️ Can a seller *withdraw* a submitted bid?

> ### 🚨 The partial-shipment ↔ single-winner contradiction
> FR-4.4 lets sellers bid partial quantities, and FR-4.6 ranks everyone by **per-kg total rate**. But §6 closes the deal by selecting *one* vendor ("Confirm Deal").
>
> Two problems:
> 1. **Ranking apples vs oranges.** A vendor offering 5,000 kg at ₹98/kg ranks "better" than one offering the full 10,000 kg at ₹100/kg — but the first only covers half the need. Pure per-kg ranking is misleading when quantities differ.
> 2. **Fulfilling the order.** If partials are allowed, the buyer almost certainly needs to *award multiple sellers* to cover the full quantity — which the single "Confirm Deal" flow doesn't support.
>
> **⚠️ DECISION NEEDED:** Either (a) drop partial bids in v1 (simplest), or (b) build multi-award deal closure with a "fill the quantity" basket UI and per-line confirmation emails. This choice ripples through ranking, counter-offers, and closure.

### 5.5 Evaluation & Counter-Offer Loop

**FR-5.1 Stage-1 sort.** At deadline, bids lock and sort lowest→highest Total Rate/kg, with the auction average shown. Expanding a card reveals full corporate + contact details.

**FR-5.2 Stage-2 universal blast.** Buyer sets one target counter-rate sent to all participants; starts a strict 24-hour timer.
- ⚠️ One counter-rate to all only makes sense if bids are comparable — reopens the partial-bid problem (FR-4.4).
- ⚠️ Is Stage 2 a *single* round, or can the buyer counter repeatedly? Source implies one round.
- ⚠️ Can the buyer counter only a *subset* of sellers? Currently all-or-nothing.

**FR-5.3 Final-2-hour urgency.** Ranks hidden, urgent push + email with the ₹ rate in the subject line.

**FR-5.4 Vendor response routing.** Accept / Reject / Submit Final Alternative Rate. **Price-drop lock:** Stage-2 rate cannot exceed the seller's Stage-1 bid (rejected with an input error).

**FR-5.5 Inactivity timeout.** No response in 24h → status **Expired**; Stage-1 bid preserved and still visible to buyer.

### 5.6 Post-Auction & Closure

**FR-6.1 Combined summary card** per vendor: Stage-1 block (qty, basic, freight, total, payment terms) + Stage-2 block (counter target + final action) + document downloads (specs + COA). No time limit on the buyer here.

**FR-6.2 Super-comparison leaderboard.** For each seller, take the *lowest* of their Stage-1 or Stage-2 total rate; sort sellers by that. (Excellent rule — keep it.)

**FR-6.3 Deep-dive profile + block.** Clicking a vendor opens full corporate footprint; a "Block Seller for this CAS Number" button permanently mutes that seller for this buyer + CAS. Historical bid data is retained with a 🔴 [Blocked] tag.

**FR-6.4 Legal settlement.** "Confirm Deal" locks the auction and emails both parties identical confirmations with corporate disclosures, employee contacts, and final commercial metrics (qty, payment terms, lead times, split pricing).
- ⚠️ **"Legally binding" claim needs legal review.** An email is not automatically an enforceable contract. Define T&Cs accepted at signup, dispute resolution, and governing jurisdiction. Loop in a lawyer.
- ⚠️ **No payment terms capture flow.** "Payment terms" and "lead times" appear in the summary but there's no field where the seller *entered* them during bidding. Where do they come from?
- 🚨 Single-winner closure conflicts with partial bids (see callout above).

### 5.7 Records & Market Feed

**FR-7.1 Sector folders.** Active / Closed / Expired-Unsuccessful, for both Purchase and Sales modules. Cards show corporate name (line 1) + user/designation (line 2).

**FR-7.2 AI market feed.** Aggregated industry news, personalized by profile activity: buyers see Price Trends + Govt Rules for their chemicals; sellers see Logistics + raw-material shifts. New users get a chronological mix until their first listing.
- ⚠️ **News source + licensing.** RSS? Paid feeds? Scraping (legal/ToS risk)? This needs a real content pipeline decision and likely a recurring cost.
- ⚠️ **Which AI, and what does it cost per user per month?** "Real-time analysis" of every profile is expensive if done naively. Consider precomputed tags + scheduled batch ranking instead.

## 6. Non-Functional Requirements (entirely absent from source doc ⚠️)

- **Security:** password policy, 2FA option, session timeout, rate limiting, encryption at rest/in transit. Sensitive data: GST certs, contact details, spec sheets, COAs.
- **Privacy / compliance:** India's **DPDP Act 2023** applies — consent, data retention, deletion rights. Define who can see whose contact info and when (identities are masked during bidding, revealed after).
- **Audit log:** immutable record of bids, counters, accepts, blocks, deal confirmations (the spec's whole "audit trail" rationale depends on this).
- **Notifications infra:** email provider (SES, etc.), push (FCM/APNs or web push), optional SMS for urgency alerts. Delivery/retry guarantees.
- **Availability & scale:** expected concurrent auctions/users; what happens to a live auction during downtime?
- **File handling:** storage, max size, antivirus scan, access control on spec/COA downloads.
- **Platform/operator tooling:** moderation, dispute queue, manual GST override, analytics dashboard.

## 7. Platform & Tech Notes

- Source says "responsive for mobile and desktop browsers" → **responsive web app, not native**. Confirm whether a PWA (installable, push-capable) is wanted.
- ⚠️ **Tech stack not specified.** Your prior chemical-trading dashboard used Apps Script + Next.js + Sheets + Vercel. This platform — with auth, real-time bidding, file storage, scheduled timers, and external APIs — likely outgrows Sheets-as-database. Worth deciding early: a real DB (Postgres), a backend for timers/notifications, and where files live.

## 8. Out of Scope (v1)

Payments/escrow, invoicing/e-way bills, logistics tracking, international trade, native apps, seller/buyer reputation ratings (beyond block), in-app messaging/chat.

## 9. Suggested MVP / Phasing

A pragmatic cut to get to a working marketplace fast:

**Phase 1 — Core loop (full-quantity only):** GST onboarding, catalog with CAS lookup, auction creation, targeting, Stage-1 blind bidding, single-winner closure, email confirmation, sector folders. **Drop partial bids and counter-offers** to sidestep the biggest unresolved design problems.

**Phase 2 — Negotiation:** Stage-2 counter-offer loop, super-comparison leaderboard, urgency workflow.

**Phase 3 — Depth:** partial bids + multi-award closure (if validated as needed), registered-network privacy, seller proactive shares, blocking nuances.

**Phase 4 — Market feed** (AI news) — lowest priority; it's a retention nicety, not core.

## Appendix A — Additional Flows Missing from the Source Spec

The source describes the happy path thoroughly but omits most reversals, dead-ends, and lifecycle exits. These are the flows that need designing. Grouped by area; 🚨 = high-impact.

### A.1 Discovery & cold start
- 🚨 **Supplier discovery / directory.** The network model assumes a buyer already knows a partner's GST number. With identities masked during bidding and no browse/search directory, a brand-new buyer has no way to find a first seller. Decide: a public supplier directory, a "Send to All" default that seeds first contact, or an onboarding-assisted matching step.
- **Bootstrapping liquidity.** How are the first sellers and buyers brought on so early auctions actually get bids? (Ops/seeding flow, not just product.)

### A.2 Authentication & account
- Login / logout / "remember me".
- Forgot-password and password reset.
- Signup verification (email link and/or phone OTP).
- 🚨 **Sub-user invitation acceptance** — does the invited employee set their own password on first login, or does the admin set it? Define first-login flow.
- 🚨 **Employee offboarding** — when a sales/purchase user is removed, who inherits their catalog items, live auctions, and submitted bids? The "one CAS per user per department" rule makes reassignment mandatory.
- Admin handover / "the admin left the company."
- Platform-initiated account suspension and the user-facing flow for it.

### A.3 Catalog management
- Edit a listed product (change grade, role declaration, mixture text).
- Delist / delete a product — blocked or warned if it has active auctions?
- 🚨 **CAS-collision resolution.** Today the alert names the colleague who owns the CAS and stops. Add a request-transfer or admin-reassign path so it isn't a dead end.

### A.4 Auction lifecycle (buyer side)
- Edit an auction after publishing (e.g., quantity typo) — allowed, and what do already-notified sellers see?
- 🚨 Cancel / withdraw an auction before close — and the notification sellers receive.
- Extend the closing deadline.
- 🚨 **Zero-bid outcome** — auto-move to Unsuccessful, offer relist, notify buyer?
- 🚨 **Bids received but never confirmed** — does it stay "Active" forever, or auto-expire after N days?
- Single-bid auction — is there still a "competition", and does Stage 2 counter-offer apply?
- **Clone / re-run** a past auction (retention driver).
- Explicit "close without award" action (move to Unsuccessful intentionally).

### A.5 Seller side
- 🚨 **Proactive material share — full creation flow.** Currently only its throttling, toggles, and bilateral gateway are described. Define the actual share-creation screen, fields, and exactly what the recipient buyer sees.
- Withdraw a submitted bid before deadline.
- 🚨 **"Ignore" reversal** — once muted, is there genuinely no way back until a new request? Confirm or add an "un-ignore."
- Editing a bid: rate revision vs. partial-quantity change interplay.

### A.6 Counter-offer & negotiation
- 🚨 **All sellers reject the counter** — does the buyer fall back to Stage-1 prices, or is the auction dead?
- Buyer cancels Stage 2 mid-countdown.
- Multiple sellers accept a counter (with partial quantities) — multi-accept handling (ties into the partial-bid problem in §5.4).
- Whether the buyer can run more than one counter round.

### A.7 Closure, deals & disputes
- 🚨 **Deal fall-through.** A confirmed deal is "locked permanently to prevent data mutation," leaving zero recourse if a party backs out off-platform. Add a dispute / cancellation / re-open path (with audit-preserving status changes rather than data deletion).
- Export a closed deal / auction history (PDF/CSV) for the buyer's and seller's accounting.

### A.8 Network management
- Edit / remove a registered partner.
- 🚨 **Register a partner not yet on the platform** — invite flow vs. silent placeholder.
- Does a company get notified (and can it object) when another firm registers it into a private network?

### A.9 Notifications & communication
- In-app **notification center** with history and read/unread state (today only transient pop-ups exist).
- 🚨 **Email unsubscribe / preferences** — legally required for non-transactional email in India; also reduces spam complaints.
- Notification preferences (real-time vs. digest; which events).

### A.10 Trust, safety & integrity
- Report a bad actor beyond blocking (to the platform operator).
- 🚨 **Fake-auction / price-fishing defense** — what stops a competitor posting sham auctions purely to harvest others' pricing? Consider limits, verification, or reputation signals.
- Seller capability verification beyond GST (does the seller actually have/produce the chemical?).

### A.11 Operator (platform-side) tooling
- Moderation queue, dispute handling, manual GST override when the API fails, account suspension, and a basic analytics dashboard (auctions, bids, completion rate, active companies). None of this exists in the source and you'll need it from day one of having real users.
